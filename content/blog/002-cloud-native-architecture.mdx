# Cloud-Native Architecture for Marketing Websites

In today's digital landscape, marketing websites need to be more than just static pages. They require dynamic content, personalization, and the ability to scale with your business needs.

## Why Cloud-Native?

Cloud-native architecture provides several advantages for marketing websites:

- **Scalability**: Handle traffic spikes during campaigns
- **Resilience**: Maintain uptime even during infrastructure issues
- **Cost-efficiency**: Pay only for what you use
- **Global reach**: Deploy close to your users worldwide

## Kubernetes and vCluster

The SOTA Marketing Stack leverages Kubernetes with vCluster to create isolated environments for each tenant, ensuring security and resource isolation while maintaining operational efficiency.

## Implementation Guide

This boilerplate includes complete Kubernetes manifests and deployment guides to help you implement this architecture in your own environment.
